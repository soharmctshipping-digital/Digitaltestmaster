# Sohar Shipping: Inland Transport Rate List (import deliveries inside Oman)

**What this is.** A lane-by-lane list of the inland transport charges Sohar Shipping has quoted for delivering import cargo inside Oman after customs clearance. It is meant as the reference the WhatsApp bot uses when it prepares an import customs clearance quotation with delivery.

**Source.** The company's own past quotations in the "SST CLAUDE" Google Drive folder (quotations dated 8 July to 19 October 2026), plus the five template rate points given by the owner. No rate has been estimated or interpolated.

**Date built.** 22 November 2026.

**All rates are subject to change due to truck availability, cargo dimensions and weight, site access, waiting time and fuel or permit conditions.**

How to read the tables:

- **Rate** is the figure to use: the owner's template where one exists; otherwise the most common figure quoted, or the most recent where no figure is more common than the others. Rates are shown in the currency quoted; USD is compared at 1 OMR = USD 2.6008.
- **Confidence**: *Owner template* (given by the owner); *Firm* (3 or more quotations within 10%); *Indicative* (2 quotations, or more with a spread above 10%); *Single quotation* (1).
- **Range seen** is in OMR equivalent. Rates exclude VAT, offloading, port and customs charges.
- Container rates are for standard road-legal, in-gauge, non-hazardous loads, one way to a single delivery point, unless the row says otherwise.

## How this was built

- Quotations examined: the 333 quotation PDFs in the Drive folder, through the earlier line-item extraction (317 with extracted lines, 16 with none). 120 of them are import quotations.
- That extraction was made for customs-clearance charges and held almost no transport lines (one usable figure), so the transport charges were read from the source PDFs.
- Read from Drive for this list: 58 import quotations. 54 of them print an inland transport figure; 4 do not.
- One further figure (SSTTA/QTN/2026/09-240, Sohar city limits) was taken from the earlier extraction without re-reading the PDF.
- Unreadable: none of the 58. Two files in the folder had already been recorded as empty (0 bytes) in the earlier extraction; one of them looks like an import ocean-freight quotation.
- Not read (61 import quotations): about 42 ocean-freight-only quotations to "Sohar, Oman" (two were sampled and both exclude inland transport, saying it can be quoted separately); 4 air imports for re-export; 9 air import quotations for Muscat, mostly exhibition cargo and duplicates (SSTTA/QTN/2026/07-154, 08-176, 09-243 and its revision, 09-246, 09-AIR01, two unnumbered August 2026 quotations and one October test quotation); 3 break-bulk quotations (fly ash to Misfah, salt to Rusayl and Sohar Industrial Area, priced per tonne rather than per truck); 3 road imports from the UAE through the land border; SSTTA/QTN/2026/08-215 (superseded by 09-217, already counted) and SSTTA/QTN/2026/10-260 (delivery to Mabelah optional). The Muscat Airport rows are therefore not complete.
- Lanes: 37 in total: 5 owner template, 1 firm, 11 indicative, 20 single quotation.
- Several "2 quotation" lanes are the same customer quoted twice (Saham, Al Amerat, Jifnain flatbed, and two of the four for Mabelah), so they show consistency rather than two independent price points.
- The machine-readable copy is `bot_build/transport_rates.json`. In it, `low` and `high` are in the same currency as `rate`; three extra keys (`earliest`, `refs`, `notes`) are added to the fields requested.
